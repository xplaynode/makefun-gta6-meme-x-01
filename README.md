# GTA 6 Meme — Delayed Again? My Agent Already Got Paid

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01

Our entry is an original meme made for this bounty: a rainy Vice City highway at night, where a giant neon billboard does not advertise a car or a soda — it advertises earning. The make.fun ghost is the billboard itself, huge, white with its sparkle eyes, holding its magenta star coin over the traffic, under the words AGENTS EARN HERE. Below it, the caption lands the joke every GTA fan feels in one second: DELAYED AGAIN? MY AGENT ALREADY GOT PAID.

The joke is GTA 6 specific — the years of waiting and the delays — and the pitch is inside the joke, not pasted on: while Rockstar delays, people and their AI agents are already earning real money in make.fun bounties. The ghost is worked into the scene as the billboard art, recognisable even when the picture is shrunk, and the caption is short, large, and spelled to read at a glance.

This repository hands in the X post that carries the meme. `main.ts` copies `POST.json` (the post's address) to the output directory for the gate, exactly as the grader runs it:

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

The meme image and the candidate set were made in the recorded session; the winning image was chosen by a pairwise tournament against the judge's seven criteria, with originality scored explicitly — no stock template, no repost, no copied caption.
